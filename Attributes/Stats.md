To determine your character's stats, roll with 3d6 in order for each one.
## Strength

Fight with a sword, bash open doors, swim. Important for fighters.
## Dexterity

Shoot a bow, balance on a ledge, sneak silently, hide. Important for thieves.
## Constitution

Hold your breath, resist poision, endure injury.
## Intelligence

Cast wizard spells, decipher runes, learn new skills. Important for wizards.
## Wisdom

Cast priest spells, detect the hidden, recognize omens, important for priests.
## Charisma

Convince creatures to e friendly, resist mental control.
## Modifiers

| Stats | Modifiers |
| ----- | --------- |
| 1-3   | -4        |
| 4-5   | -3        |
| 6-7   | -2        |
| 8-9   | -1        |
| 10-11 | 0         |
| 12-13 | +1        |
| 14-15 | +2        |
| 16-17 | +3        |
| 18+   | +4        |
## Hit Points
One roll of their class's hit points die + their Constitution modifier (minimum 1). 
## Armor Class (AC)
AC is a type of difficulty class that shows how tough is to injure you, whether because you are agile or you wear armour. Your class determines the type of armour you can wear.

Your armour class (AC) is 10 + your [[Stats#Dexterity]] modifier
